The Beginning of Pragati

Pragati began with a simple vision: to create a dining experience that celebrates the rich culinary heritage of India while embracing modern sophistication and innovation.

Founded with a passion for authentic flavors, Pragati has become a beloved destination for Indian dining on Main Road, Kakinada.

What started as a small family restaurant has evolved into an acclaimed culinary institution, known for its exceptional service, elegant ambiance, and masterfully crafted dishes.
